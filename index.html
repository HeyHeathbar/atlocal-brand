Brand System

The official logo, colors, type and tagline for every atLocal deliverable.

AI tools and collaborators: read /START_HERE.md before making anything branded. It holds every rule and value. Don't infer the brand from any website.

Name

atLocal

Always written atLocal: lowercase "a", capital "L", one word, even at the start of a sentence. Never AtLocal, Atlocal, ATLOCAL or atlocal. The bracketed at[local] appears only in the logo files.

Tagline

Master

Your Story
Amplified

Adapts to the audience

Your businessstorepracticeorganizationfoundationnonprofit
Amplified

Your Story Amplified is the master, with "Story" capitalized and no periods. "Your" and "Amplified" never change. When we know the audience, the middle word matches it: business (businesses), store (retail), practice (healthcare and professional practices), organization (organizations and associations), foundation, nonprofit. Pick one per piece and keep it.
